STUDIO 5000 // MAINROUTINE STAR-DELTA STARTER

PLC-5000 Industrial Ladder Logic Motor Control Simulator

Browser-based industrial PLC training and logic-testing simulator modeled after Studio 5000 ladder environments. Built to bridge the gap between textbook relay logic and real-world troubleshooting, enabling technicians and students to inspect live rung power flow, toggle virtual field inputs, and verify timing and interlocking behavior without physical PLC hardware.

Key Test Scenarios
  • Live Rung Execution & Seal-In Logic: Trace real-time power flow across XIC/XIO contacts, latching seal-in branches, and output coils (OTE).
  • Star-Delta Starter Sequencing: Test automated transition timing (TON) and electrical/software interlocking between Main, Star, and Delta outputs.
  • Virtual I/O & Fault Testing: Force pushbuttons, overload trip contacts, and emergency stop inputs to observe how safety interlocks drop out active rungs.

APP_02 // MOTOR STARTERS Built From Scratch with AI Assistance
IEC 60617 // SCHEMATIC ENGINE 3-PHASE & CONTROL CIRCUIT

Motor Control Simulator

Interactive IEC 60617 schematic and control-circuit simulator modeling three-phase motor starter topologies. Designed from field maintenance experience, it visualizes how control transformers, pushbuttons, auxiliary contacts, and thermal overload relays interact during normal startup, direction reversal, and live fault conditions.

Key Test Scenarios
  • Starter Topologies: Switch between Direct-On-Line (DOL), Forward-Reverse interlocking, and Wye-Delta reduced-voltage starter circuits.
  • Contactor & Auxiliary State Tracing: Observe real-time energization of KM1/KM2/KM3 coils, holding contacts, and mechanical/electrical interlocks.
  • Simulated Fault Troubleshooting: Inject thermal overload (OL) trips, open control fuses, or coil failures to practice systematic circuit isolation.
